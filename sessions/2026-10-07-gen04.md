## Mechanism

Immersive observer callbacks share a FIFO queue of six with per-block deduplication and finite transient retries. A page generation and AbortController discard work after stop, re-enable, and SPA URL navigation. Navigating to an empty page clears its polling timer.

All translation stream ports share a service-worker budget of six active provider paths with a finite waiting queue. Disconnect, explicit abort, and same-port replacement cancel the provider signal. An aborted active request keeps its lease until the provider path actually settles; cancelled waiting requests never start. Provider fallback, late chunks, and cache writes stop after cancellation.

A new quick or deep selection owns its card generation. Closing, Escape, an outside click, and whitelist dismissal cancel pending selection work; late quick/deep responses cannot reopen or overwrite a later card. Completed/error streams and failed posts disconnect their port.

Input enhancement binds a request and undo snapshot to one input. Focus changes, stop, and external input cancel it. An interrupted partial result restores the original only while the input still contains the stream's last applied text; newer manual/programmatic edits survive. Switching targets clears the prior undo action. Disable clears pending blur/undo callbacks and ports.
